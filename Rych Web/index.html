About Me

Someone people find easy to talk to — and even easier to open up to.

I've been a talker since I was a kid — endlessly curious about people, always the one starting a conversation. Even during the pandemic, when everyone else's world got smaller, mine somehow grew: I ended up with close friends scattered across cities, even countries, built entirely through conversation.

That instinct followed me through school. In middle school, I had my own close circle, but I made sure I never stayed boxed into it — I moved easily between groups instead of picking just one. In high school, that same ease talking to anyone (even seniors two years above me) led me somewhere unexpected: I became the head of my school's Kerohanian organization. Around the same time, classmates started treating me less like a friend and more like someone they could actually confide in.

That's the thread I'm carrying forward: I listen first, then find the words that make one side understood by the other. It's a habit that doesn't stay in one room — it walks into any environment I'm in, whether that's a classroom, a team, or someday, the space between a developer and the people they're building for.
